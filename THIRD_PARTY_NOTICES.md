# Third-party notices

The hardware-specific SMC sensor name catalog adapts data from [Stats](https://github.com/exelban/stats), `Modules/Sensors/values.swift`, lookup conventions in `Modules/Sensors/readers.swift`, and the exact platform scopes in `Kit/plugins/SystemKit.swift`, pinned to revision 0c0e551e199938a92af963c25a15a3ca1aef7764. Physical core labels are expressed as sensor labels; these community mappings do not establish a verified physical component identity. Wildcard labels retain their native slot suffix, rather than a dynamically assigned inventory ordinal. No upstream transport or application implementation is incorporated.
